How it is worked out

Delegate to us and we return 40% of the commission your delegation earns, every week. What you get depends on your own stake, not on how many others take part.

payout = your stake × staking APR × commission × 40%
commission — 20% on Celestia, 5% on Cosmos Hub

A round runs Monday to Sunday, with one reading a day. Your basis for the round is the lowest of those seven, and payouts go out on Sunday.

Miss a day and the round pays nothing. Delegating just before a reading and leaving right after earns zero. Delegate mid-week and that round pays nothing either — you join the round starting the following Monday and are paid at the end of it, on the Sunday.
